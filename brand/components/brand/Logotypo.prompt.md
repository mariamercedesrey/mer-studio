The "Agente Mamá AI" client logotype (Outfit 300 + 500, terracotta "AI") — pair with `Icon` for that client's lockup in the marquee.

```jsx
<div style={{display:'flex',gap:3.3,alignItems:'center'}}>
  <Icon type="negative" style={{width:39.683,height:39.683}} />
  <Logotypo type="horizontal" />
</div>
```

- `type`: `horizontal` (one line) or `vertical` (stacked, default).
- Text props `text1/text2/text3` default to "Agente" / "Mamá" / "AI".
